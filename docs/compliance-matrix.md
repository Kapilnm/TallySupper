# India compliance discovery matrix (dated 30 Sep 2026)

This is a **scope map**, not a legal opinion or a complete list of every Indian compliance. Applicability must be determined for each entity, State/UT, GSTIN, tax year, transaction and notification. The current prototype does not calculate or file statutory returns.

| Area | What a production accounting product must know | Prototype status | Control / verification needed before release |
|---|---|---|---|
| Books of account & double entry | Accrual/cash policy, chart of accounts, source documents, receipts/payments/sales/purchases, opening balances, corrections, period closing and record export. Company requirements include Companies Act sections 128–134 and applicable rules; other entity types have different requirements. | Basic balanced double-entry vouchers and reports. | CA-approved opening/closing workflow, accounting-policy configuration, migration, decimal/paise-safe arithmetic and audit of every posting path. |
| Financial reporting framework | Companies Act Schedule III and notified AS/Ind AS requirements apply according to legal form, listing/size/sector and other tests. Proprietorship/partnership/LLP/company/NBFC presentations are not one universal template. | Basic TB, P&L, balance sheet; no statutory formatting. | Entity eligibility matrix, MCA/ICAI rule packs, CA-reviewed presentation and disclosure checklist. |
| GST registration & invoice | GSTIN/state, registration type, supplier/recipient facts, place of supply, invoice series/date, HSN/SAC, quantity/unit, taxable value, tax components, reverse charge, exports/SEZ, exemptions, notes and supporting evidence. | Demo state-based tax split, one goods item per voucher. | Current CGST/SGST/IGST notifications, transaction-type and place-of-supply rules, GSTIN/HSN validation source, rounding tests and exception handling. |
| GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B | Tax-period outward supply detail and summary liability/ITC; monthly vs QRMP frequency; nil-return situations; amendments/notes; portal status, reconciliation and notifications. GST Portal general dates are 11/13 for GSTR-1 and 20/22/24 for GSTR-3B according to filing frequency/State, subject to extensions. | Books-side tax summary and indicative calendar only. | Rule-driven calendar from official portal/notifications; compare books to portal data; validate current return schema. Never mark a local preview “filed.” |
| Input tax credit / IMS | Eligibility, supplier reporting, GSTR-2B/IMS actions, reversals, blocked credits, RCM and amendments. Booked input tax is not synonymous with eligible credit. | No GSTR-2B/IMS feed or eligibility engine. | Official import/portal integration, reconciliation states, rule versioning and practitioner review. |
| E-invoicing | Notified-turnover applicability, taxpayer exemptions, invoice schema, IRN/QR, cancellation/amendment and reporting time window. Current research distinguishes the ₹5 crore notified threshold from the 30-day reporting restriction for AATO ₹10 crore and above (effective 1 Apr 2025). | Not connected; no IRN or e-invoice is generated. | Re-check current notification/IRP advisory per release; approved IRP/GSP integration; avoid claiming an invoice is valid before IRN where legally required. |
| E-way bill | Covered goods movements, consignment value, route/mode/transporter/vehicle/distance, validity, exceptions and State-specific provisions. ₹50,000 is a general rule threshold, not a universal exemption boundary. | Not implemented. | Current CGST Rule 138 / State notifications, portal API schemas and movement-level validations. |
| GST electronic records & retention | Backup/restore, source-document linkage, record layout, audit trail, retention and production of records. Section 36 generally uses 72 months from the annual-return due date; ongoing proceedings can extend retention. | Local JSON and a readable event list; not tamper-proof. | Retention policy by legal hold/statute; verifiable backups, restoration drills and tamper-evident/append-only audit records. |
| Income tax / TDS / TCS | Payee and payer facts, payment/credit trigger, threshold, current rate, residency, PAN, certificates, challans, statements/forms, corrections, deposit date, tax-audit and year transition. | TDS ledger and “setup required” status only; no tax computation. | Dated rule packs from Income-tax Act, 2025 and Rules, 2026; old Act retained for pre-1 Apr 2026 events; review all rates/forms/due dates with a tax professional. |
| 2026 direct-tax transition | Official e-Filing guidance says the earlier of credit/payment through 31 Mar 2026 remains under the 1961 Act; events from 1 Apr 2026 use the 2025 Act. New withholding references include section 392 (salary), 393 (other TDS) and 394 (TCS). | Research documented only. | Persist legal regime and section/table item on each transaction; preserve old-period forms and correction path; test March/April boundary cases. |
| Company audit trail | Companies (Accounts) Rules and Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules prescribe accounting-software audit-trail/edit-log controls for covered companies. Scope, commencement, preservation and reporting must be verified against amendments. | Posted vouchers are read-only in UI, but the browser event list is not legally tamper-proof. | Append-only event store, actor/timestamp/before-after, tamper detection, retention, privileged-access controls and CA/statutory-auditor review. |
| Statutory audit / CA support | Audit evidence, working papers, source-document indexing, confirmations, reconciliations, risk assessment, review sign-off and applicable Standards on Auditing. | View-only posting details and a local event list. | Product may prepare audit evidence and checklists; it must not present itself as auditor, certify accounts or issue a CA opinion. |
| Payroll & employee deductions | Salary TDS under current law, EPF, ESI, Professional Tax, labour requirements, state-specific registrations/thresholds, employee records and returns. | Not implemented. | Jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction applicability and payroll-specialist review before any payroll module ships. |
| MSME / supplier terms | Udyam/MSME status, vendor aging, payment due dates and disclosures/interest implications where applicable. | Generic FIFO open-bill view; no Udyam or statutory-interest logic. | Verify MSMED Act/rules, entity status and due-date/payment computation with current law and CA. |
| Privacy & security | Customer/vendor/employee personal data; notice, purpose, access, correction, retention, deletion, breach response, data processors and backup protection. DPDP Act/Rules have staged commencement. | No login, encryption, role system, cloud processing or DPDP workflow. | Track Gazette commencement by provision, apply privacy-by-design, encrypt production data/backups and implement rights/security processes. |
| Bank, digital payment and exports | Bank file schemas, reconciliation, payment authorization, UPI/card settlement, payment-data safety, export/foreign currency and exchange differences. | Manual local cash/bank ledger only; no bank or payment connection. | Read-only import and reconciliation first; production integrations require provider/contract/security review and explicit user consent. |

## Release gates for statutory features

Before a tax or statutory module is represented as production-ready:

1. Identify authoritative primary sources (Act, Rules, Gazette notification, official portal schema/advisory) and record retrieval date, effective date, affected transaction/tax period and superseded version.
2. Convert every rule into a dated testable rule pack with positive, negative, boundary, exception and correction scenarios; do not silently apply the latest rule to historical periods.
3. Obtain review by an Indian CA/tax/GST professional for the intended entity and geography; retain reviewer, date, scope and caveats.
4. Validate sample files against official offline utilities/sandbox portals where available; distinguish prepared, uploaded, accepted, filed and paid states.
5. Keep human approval, exportable evidence and a safe rollback path. An AI suggestion must never post a statutory entry or file a return without explicit review.
